# Particle Orb

The orb is a deterministic Fibonacci sphere rather than a random cloud. This provides even coverage at every quality tier and makes the glowing silhouette read as a continuous shell.

Primary tuning:

- Radius: `1.88`
- Rim power: `2.65`
- Rim brightness: `1.32`
- Center opacity: `0.006`
- Edge opacity: `0.62`
- Scroll expansion: `1.58`
- Final survivor set: `18%`

The outer halo uses a separate smaller point budget and brighter rim treatment. During the scroll story, sphere positions expand, destabilize, and blend into deterministic field positions; seeded particle membership keeps the transition reversible.

A third signal layer places a small budget of larger nodes just above the primary shell. These nodes use hot pearl-lime cores, soft additive falloff, seeded size variation, and independent low-frequency twinkle timing. Their center visibility is intentionally higher than the structural shell so sparse points remain legible inside the silhouette, while depth testing keeps the AV mark clean in front.

At rest, the shell never becomes static. Rotating coordinate fields drive broad lobes, traveling crests, fine surface waves, tangential particle drift, and a low-amplitude breath. The frequencies are deliberately separated so highlights continuously migrate around the entire silhouette instead of merely pulsing in place.

The upper hemisphere uses directional brightness suppression to prevent a persistent top bloom hotspot while preserving the particle silhouette.
